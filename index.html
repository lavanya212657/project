Why Local Volunteer Finder?

Finding a way to help shouldn't be difficult. Local Volunteer Finder makes it easier for people to discover volunteer opportunities based on where they live, what they care about, and when they are available.

Our goal is simple: connect people who want to help with organizations that are making a difference.
